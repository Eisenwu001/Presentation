STRENGTHS & BLINDSPOTS

DBSCAN excels at discovering complex shapes without guessing K, but requires disciplined parameter tuning.

✓ ASSETS / PROS

  • + No Need to Guess K: Clusters emerge organically from spatial distribution.
  • + Arbitrary Geometries: Connects non-linear crescents, serpentine trails, and rings.
  • + Outlier Immunity: Built-in noise rejection protects models from dirty data.

× BLINDSPOTS / CONS

  • ! Varying Density Dilemma: A single global ε fails on multi-density sets.
